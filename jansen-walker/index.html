Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The body pose (x, ride height, pitch) is replayed from the Dyad JansenLocomotion solve — 6 states, compliant ground contact, regularised Coulomb friction. Each leg's eleven bars are recomputed in the browser from the crank angle, so leg geometry costs no bandwidth.

Crank speed, friction, mass and terrain amplitude are baked into the export: changing them needs a new solve. Re-export with julia --project=. scripts/export_web.jl.
